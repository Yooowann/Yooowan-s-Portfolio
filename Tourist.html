The Philippines

Top 3 Tropical Spots to Explore

Boracay Island

World-famous for its powdery, bone-white sand beaches, vibrant nightlife, and breathtaking sunsets.

El Nido, Palawan

A paradise of dramatic limestone cliffs towering over hidden lagoons, secret beaches, and turquoise waters.

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

An extraordinary landscape of over 1,200 perfectly cone-shaped hills that turn cocoa-brown during the dry season.
