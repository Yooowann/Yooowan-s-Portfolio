About Me

Personal Profile

My name is Yuwan Raymundo, born and raised in the Philippines. Since I was young, I have always been curious about how things work and enjoyed learning new skills. My experiences growing up have helped shape me into the person I am today.

During my childhood, I spent most of my time exploring my interests, spending time with my family, and making memories with friends. I enjoyed activities such as playing video games, listening to music, and learning about technology. These interests influenced my decision to pursue a career related to Technology.

I am currently a college student taking up a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology. Throughout my studies, I have learned valuable skills in programming, web development, and problem-solving. Although I face challenges along the way, I continue to improve myself and gain knowledge that will help me achieve my goals.

Aside from academics, I enjoy playing guitar, watching movies and anime, and exploring new ideas. These hobbies allow me to express my creativity and relax during my free time.

My goal in life is to become a successful IT professional one day and use my skills to create useful technologies that can help others. I believe that continuous learning, determination, and hard work will help me achieve my dreams.
